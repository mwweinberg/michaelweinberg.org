It's that time of the <s>year</s> triennial cycle again...

I just submitted a request to renew the exemption that allows breaking Digital Rights Management on 3D printers in order to use third party printing materials.  

During the last cycle I wrote a [quick summary of the process](https://michaelweinberg.org/blog/2023/07/13/1201-kickoff/), and everything from then still applies today. In a just world, this will be a straightforward renewal of an unopposed renewal request.

Do we live in a just world, at least in the exceptionally narrow context of renewing 1201 exemption petitions regarding the use of third party printing materials? 

Stay tuned.....
